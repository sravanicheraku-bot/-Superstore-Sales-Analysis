# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis Using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Superstore Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional business performance using Microsoft Power BI.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and data visualization.

This analysis helps businesses understand sales trends, identify profitable products, evaluate regional performance, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Understand sales performance across different regions.
- Analyze customer segments and purchasing patterns.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Analyze shipping modes and their contribution to sales.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Cleaning
- Data Transformation
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## 📂 Dataset Description

The Superstore dataset contains transactional sales information.

**Important columns include:**

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Order ID | Unique order identification |
| Order Date | Date of customer order |
| Ship Date | Date of shipment |
| Customer ID | Unique customer identification |
| Customer Name | Customer name |
| Segment | Customer segment |
| Region | Business region |
| State | State of customer |
| Category | Product category |
| Sub-Category | Product sub-category |
| Product Name | Product name |
| Sales | Total sales amount |
| Quantity | Number of products sold |
| Discount | Discount offered |
| Profit | Profit generated |
| Ship Mode | Shipping method |

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation

Data preparation was performed using Power Query.

Key activities included:

- Checked and handled missing values.
- Removed duplicate records where applicable.
- Verified data types.
- Formatted date columns.
- Checked numerical columns.
- Prepared the dataset for analysis.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on the following business metrics:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Discount
- Profit Margin

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Analyzed overall sales performance.
- Compared sales across different time periods.
- Identified monthly and yearly trends.

### 2. Regional Analysis
- Compared sales and profit across regions.
- Identified high-performing and low-performing regions.
- Evaluated regional business performance.

### 3. Category & Sub-Category Analysis
- Compared sales across product categories.
- Identified top-selling sub-categories.
- Analyzed product profitability.

### 4. Customer Segment Analysis
- Evaluated sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- Compared customer segment contributions.

### 5. Discount & Profit Analysis
- Explored the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Identified products and categories affected by higher discounts.

### 6. Shipping Analysis
- Compared different shipping modes.
- Evaluated their contribution to sales and orders.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis helps answer important business questions:

1. Which region generates the highest sales and profit?
2. Which product category performs best?
3. Which sub-category generates the highest profit?
4. How do discounts affect profitability?
5. Which customer segment contributes the most sales?
6. During which months are sales highest?
7. Which shipping mode is commonly used?

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Focus on improving the performance of low-profit product categories.
- Review discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Strengthen sales strategies in underperforming regions.
- Identify high-value customer segments and improve customer engagement.
- Use monthly sales trends for better inventory and business planning.
